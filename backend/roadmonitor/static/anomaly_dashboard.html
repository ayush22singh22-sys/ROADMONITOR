How the score is calculated

Score = 45% × severity + 20% × trip evidence + 20% × nearby POI + 10% × road class + 5% × vulnerable users.

Severity blends event-severity p95 (70%) and mean (30%). Trip evidence uses distinct trips away from trip boundaries, capped at 3. POI distance follows the road network within 250 m; POIs more than 100 m from that network are ignored.

Weights and distance limits are configured server-side. These are provisional rules, not calibrated probabilities; validate against field inspections before making repair decisions.
